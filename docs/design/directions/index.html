CampusOS design directions

Three different looks for Siam to choose from · 25 Nov 2026

Each direction shows the same three screens with the same data: Today, the class-rep update form and the assignments list, for Siti (TKE, class rep of TKE-205) on Wednesday 25 November at 10:25 WIB. Struktur Data moved from Ruang 204 to 305; the afternoon lab is cancelled. Pick one, or name the parts to mix ("A's board, C's colours"). Research behind them: docs/design/research.md.

A · Papan Jadwal

Your day reads like a station departure board: time, class, room, and a status column. A moved room looks like a changed platform; a cancelled class like a cancelled train.

Designer's recommendation
Inspired by
SBB and KAI timetables (time left, platform right, status in its own column), Google Calendar's "now" line. Anti-reference: My Study Life's navy cards.
Palette
Board navy #0E1B2CSignal yellow #FFC72CCancel red #B3261EGround #F3F5F8Muted #4A5868
Fonts
Barlow Semi Condensed for times and rooms (built from road and rail signage, narrow tabular numerals), Barlow for text.
Cards
No cards. Full-width rows with hairlines, like a board. The class in progress is the one inverted row. Status flags are square-cornered blocks with a word and icon.
Signature move
The status column. Room changes and cancellations appear exactly where students already look for "platform" and "delay", and the rep's form previews the board row they are about to change.
Kept from the roll
From the Crouwel grid: the column grid stays visible and strict. From monochrome product pages: yellow and red only when something changed.
Risk
Dense. Needs real alignment discipline in Tailwind (grid columns, tabular numerals) or it looks like a spreadsheet.

B · Benang Emas

The day is one gold thread through your classes, taken from Aceh songket cloth. Where something changed, the thread carries a red knot.

Inspired by
Structured's day spine, Google Calendar's now line, and the colours of Aceh songket: deep red and aubergine cloth with gold thread.
Palette
Songket red #9E1B32Thread gold #A0760AAubergine ink #241019Ground #F6F3F5Done green #1F6B4F
Fonts
Lexend throughout: wide letters designed for reading proficiency, which helps students reading in a second language.
Cards
Almost none. Classes hang off the thread as text; only the class in progress sits on a raised white panel. Rounded, soft, warm.
Signature move
The thread is filled gold up to now and pale after it, so the day's progress is visible at a glance. Changes are red knots on it.
Kept from the roll
From dance notation: length is duration, so longer classes take more thread. From the vertical feed: one thing (now) owns the focus.
Risk
Most distinctive and most local, but the thread must stay a timeline and not become decoration; dark mode needs care to stay warm without looking like a night mode for a casino.

C · Beranda

Feels like the apps students already open every day: a bold colour header that greets you and puts the next room front and centre, with round shortcuts and friendly rounded cards below.

Inspired by
Access by KAI (greeting band, overlapping card, round service icons), Gojek (one committed colour, heavy rounded numbers), Todoist (entry by tapping parts of the answer).
Palette
Sea teal #00696BSun #FFB400Red #B42318Ground #F1F5F5Ink #0F2222
Fonts
Rubik: slightly rounded corners, heavy weights for the room number and countdowns, friendly at small sizes.
Cards
Big soft cards (18–22px radius) on a tinted ground, one hero card overlapping the header. A sun-yellow "sticker" marks a change.
Signature move
The hero card: the room you need right now in 42px type, with a tilted sticker when it changed. The rep form is a bottom sheet of three round "services".
Kept from the roll
From the vertical feed: the current class owns the top of the screen. From monochrome product pages: one colour owns the shell, the rest stays quiet.
Risk
Most familiar, so least memorable; a teal super-app look can drift toward generic. Easiest to build and the fastest for new users to trust.
